# How to Start Your First Stream

Ready to go live? This guide will walk you through the essential steps to prepare your scene and start broadcasting with LiveLens.

---

## 🏗️ Step 1: Prepare Your Scene (Add Sources)

Before starting the stream, you need to add content to your canvas. Without sources, your viewers will only see a black screen.

1. On the **Home Screen**, look for the **Plus (+)** icon in the Sources panel.
2. Tap it to open the **Source Selection** menu.
3. Add the elements you want in your stream:
    - **Screen Capture**: To stream your gameplay or other apps.
    - **Camera**: To show your face (Front or Back camera).
    - **Text**: For titles, social media handles, or overlays.
    - **Image/GIF**: For logos, borders, or animated stickers.
4. **Arrange your layout**: Drag and resize the sources on the canvas until your stream looks exactly how you want it.

---

## 📡 Step 2: Choose Your Destination

Once your scene is ready, tap the **"Start Streaming"** button in the Controls panel (bottom-right).

### 📺 Streaming to YouTube
YouTube requires a broadcast event to be created before sending data.
- **Login**: Ensure you are logged into YouTube in **Settings → Account**.
- **Schedule Event**: Use the **"Create Schedule"** button on the home screen or inside the stream setup.
- **Select & Go Live**: Choose your scheduled event from the list and tap "Start Stream".

### 📺 Streaming to Twitch
Twitch is optimized for fast, direct streaming.
- **Direct Login**: Log in via **Settings → Account**. LiveLens will automatically fetch your stream key and allow you to update your title/category instantly.
- **Custom RTMP**: If you prefer not to log in, you can manually paste your RTMP URL and Stream Key in the "Custom" tab.

### 🌐 Other Platforms (Facebook, Kick, etc.)
- You can use the **Direct Login** for Facebook and Kick to manage metadata and keys automatically.
- For any other platform, simply select the **CUSTOM** tab, enter your Server URL and Stream Key, and you're good to go.

---

## 🚀 Step 3: Go Live!

1. After selecting your platform and configuring the details, tap the **"START STREAM"** button.
2. Check the **Status Indicator** at the top-right of the control panel:
    - **CONNECTING**: The app is handshaking with the server.
    - **LIVE (Green)**: You are now broadcasting to the world! 🚀
3. **Monitor your stream**: Keep an eye on the **FPS** and **Bitrate** counters to ensure your connection remains stable.

---

## 💡 Pro-Tips for a Better Stream

- **Audio Check**: Use the **Audio Mixer** to ensure your Mic and Game volume levels are balanced (don't let them hit the red bars!).
- **Internet Stability**: Streaming requires a strong upload speed. Use Wi-Fi or a stable 5G connection for the best results.
- **Battery**: Streaming is resource-intensive. Keep your device plugged into a charger if you plan to stream for a long time.
