# Developer & Studio Information

## Meet the Developer

LiveLens is developed and led by **Arpan Das**, an independent creator and streamer based in **Kolkata, West Bengal, India**. 

The app was born out of Arpan's firsthand experience as a mobile streamer. Tired of the limitations, watermarks, and locked-down features of existing mobile streaming apps, he spent several years self-teaching the complexities of **OpenGL ES pipelines**, video encoding, and mobile rendering to build a professional-grade alternative.

> **"Built by a streamer, for streamers."**
> LiveLens is a solo project, built through countless late nights around a full-time commitment, driven by a passion for mobile content creation.

---

## 🎨 Bycon Studio

LiveLens is the flagship project of **Bycon Studio**, an independent, creator-owned entity focused on building tools that prioritize user freedom and privacy.

### Our Philosophy
- **No Ads, No Watermarks**: Your content belongs to you. We believe the app should never interfere with your broadcast.
- **Privacy First**: No forced account creation and no data harvesting.
- **Empty Canvas Workflow**: We provide the tools; you provide the creativity. We don't force you into predefined templates.
- **Community Funded**: Instead of aggressive monetization, LiveLens relies on community support through donations (UPI/PayPal) to remain sustainable and free for everyone.
