# LiveLens --- Sponsorship Benefits

## Purpose

LiveLens is an independently developed mobile streaming and recording
application focused on giving creators more freedom and control over
their streaming workflow. It uses an **OBS-style, empty-canvas
workflow**, allowing creators to build their own scenes instead of being
restricted to predefined layouts. fileciteturn22file2L199-L207

The sponsorship program is intended to support continued development,
real-device testing, compatibility work, and the wider release of
LiveLens while providing meaningful visibility to the sponsoring
company.

## What the Sponsor Receives

### 1. Splash Screen Visibility

The sponsor can receive dedicated branding visibility on the LiveLens
splash/startup screen.

The exact visual treatment can be agreed upon by both parties.

### 2. Settings Screen Visibility

A dedicated sponsor banner or card can be displayed inside the LiveLens
Settings screen.

The sponsor may provide its own approved design, subject to technical
and UI requirements.

### 3. Clickable Sponsor Placement

The Settings-screen sponsor placement can be clickable and can direct
users to the sponsor's **official website** or another mutually agreed
official destination.

### 4. Official Partner Positioning

Depending on the final agreement, the sponsor may be presented as an
**Official Partner of LiveLens**.

The exact wording, branding, and placement will be agreed upon before
implementation.

## Sponsor Creative Assets

The sponsor may provide:

-   Logo
-   Brand name
-   Banner design
-   Promotional artwork
-   Approved brand messaging
-   Official website URL

LiveLens may make reasonable technical adjustments such as sizing,
cropping, spacing, or formatting when required for proper integration.

## Exclusivity

Exclusivity is **not automatically included**.

### Non-Exclusive Sponsorship

LiveLens may work with other sponsors at the same time. The sponsor
receives the specific visibility and benefits defined in its agreement.

### Exclusive Sponsorship

The sponsor may become the exclusive sponsor within an agreed category
or scope.

Because exclusivity restricts LiveLens from accepting other sponsorship
opportunities, its scope and compensation should be negotiated
separately and written clearly in the final agreement.

## Sponsorship Duration

The preferred structure is a **one-year sponsorship agreement**.

If both parties want to continue after the agreed period, a new
agreement can be negotiated. Compensation and benefits may be reviewed
and renegotiated for each additional year.

A multi-year relationship should therefore not automatically lock both
parties into identical terms for every year.

## What Sponsorship Does Not Provide

Unless specifically agreed in writing, sponsorship does **not** provide:

-   Ownership of LiveLens
-   Ownership of Bycon Studio
-   Equity in the project
-   Source-code ownership
-   Intellectual property ownership
-   Control over LiveLens development
-   Control over the product roadmap
-   Automatic exclusivity
-   Permanent sponsorship rights

Sponsorship is a partnership for visibility and project support, not an
investment or transfer of ownership.

## Product Independence

LiveLens remains independently developed.

The sponsor does not automatically receive control over:

-   Product roadmap
-   Feature decisions
-   Technical architecture
-   Source code
-   Release decisions
-   User data
-   Community management

Any special rights or obligations must be explicitly agreed upon in the
final sponsorship agreement.

## User Experience

LiveLens is designed around a creator-focused philosophy including **no
forced account, no advertisements, no watermark, and no subscription
requirement for core use**. fileciteturn22file0L43-L70

Sponsor visibility is therefore intended to be a clearly defined
partnership placement rather than turning the application into an
advertising platform.

## What Sponsorship Supports

Sponsorship funding can help support areas such as:

-   New development hardware
-   Real-device compatibility testing
-   Graphics and rendering compatibility testing
-   Stability testing
-   Development and infrastructure costs
-   Preparation for wider Android release
-   Future platform development and research

## Final Agreement

This document describes the proposed sponsorship structure and benefits.
It is **not the final sponsorship agreement**.

Before a sponsorship begins, both parties should agree on:

-   Sponsorship amount
-   Sponsorship duration
-   Sponsor placement
-   Creative assets
-   Clickable destination
-   Exclusivity or non-exclusivity
-   Renewal terms
-   Any additional promotional obligations
-   Any special rights or restrictions

The final terms should be documented in a mutually agreed sponsorship
agreement.

## Summary

A LiveLens sponsor can receive:

-   Splash Screen visibility
-   Dedicated Settings Screen visibility
-   Clickable access to the sponsor's official website
-   Ability to provide custom sponsor creative
-   Potential **Official Partner** positioning
-   Clearly defined sponsorship benefits for the agreed period

In return, sponsorship helps LiveLens continue development, real-device
testing, compatibility work, and preparation for wider release.

**LiveLens remains independently owned and developed.**
